About RevenueForge

Built by an engineer who got tired of watching "AI growth" tools spam, scrape and lie — and decided to build the honest version.

Our story

From internal system to product

RevenueForge began as a private system: one engineer automating his own business development under strict rules — no spam, no fabricated claims, every action approved by a human, every step logged.

The system worked because it was disciplined, not because it was aggressive. That discipline became the product.

  • Research before contact, always
  • Scoring before effort, always
  • Approval before action, always
